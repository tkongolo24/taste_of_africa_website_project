Moroccan Tagine

The tagine is both the name of the dish and the conical clay pot it cooks in. Meat gently stews in its own steam with preserved lemon, saffron, and ras el hanout, a tradition shaped by Berber, Arab, and Andalusian influences.

Ingredients

Servings: 4
  • 800 g lamb shoulder, cubed
  • 2 onions, grated
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tsp ras el hanout
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 pinch saffron threads
  • 150 g dried prunes
  • 1 preserved lemon
  • 2 tbsp toasted almonds

Instructions

Step 1: Marinate
  1. Toss the lamb with the garlic, ras el hanout, cinnamon, and a little oil.
  2. Rest for at least 1 hour.
Step 2: Slow cook
  1. Layer the onions in the tagine, place the lamb on top, and add the saffron with 1 cup of water.
  2. Cover and cook on low heat for 1.5 to 2 hours until tender.
Step 3: Finish
  1. Add the prunes and preserved lemon and cook for 20 more minutes.
  2. Top with toasted almonds and serve with couscous or bread.
