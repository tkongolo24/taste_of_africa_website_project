A Brief History of African Cuisine

African cuisine is thousands of years old. Ancient grains like sorghum, millet, and teff were cultivated long before many of today's staples arrived. Trade routes across the Sahara and the Indian Ocean brought spices and new techniques, while crops such as maize, tomatoes, and chilies arrived after the 1500s and became core ingredients.

The transatlantic slave trade carried African cooking traditions to the Americas, shaping Southern US, Caribbean, and Brazilian food. Today, African chefs and flavors are reshaping menus around the world.
