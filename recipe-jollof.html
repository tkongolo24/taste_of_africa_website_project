Jollof Rice

Jollof is a beloved one-pot rice dish across West Africa, said to trace back to the Wolof people of Senegambia. Nigeria and Ghana famously debate whose version is best. It is built on tomatoes, onions, and scotch bonnet peppers.

Ingredients

Servings: 4
  • 3 cups long-grain parboiled rice
  • 4 ripe tomatoes, blended
  • 2 red bell peppers, blended
  • 1 scotch bonnet pepper
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste
  • 3 cups chicken stock
  • 1 tsp thyme, curry powder, and salt each

Instructions

Step 1: Make the pepper base
  1. Blend the tomatoes, bell peppers, and scotch bonnet until smooth.
  2. Simmer the blend in a pot until it reduces and the raw smell is gone, about 15 minutes.
Step 2: Fry the stew
  1. Heat oil, fry the sliced onions until golden, then add the tomato paste.
  2. Pour in the reduced pepper blend and fry for 10 minutes. Season with thyme, curry, and salt.
Step 3: Cook the rice
  1. Rinse the rice, add it to the stew with the hot stock, and stir once.
  2. Cover tightly, cook on low heat for 30 minutes, then rest 10 minutes before serving.
