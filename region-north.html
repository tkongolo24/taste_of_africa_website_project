North African Cuisine

Explore the aromatic spices, traditional dishes, and rich culinary heritage of North Africa.

Food Culture

North African cooking blends Berber, Arab, Mediterranean, and Ottoman influences. Meals are known for their fragrant spices, rich flavours, and communal dining traditions. Couscous is one of the region's most recognised traditional foods.

Staples

Couscous, wheat bread, olives, dates, preserved lemons, chickpeas, and lamb are important ingredients in many North African cuisines.

Cooking Techniques

Slow cooking in a tagine pot, steaming couscous, baking traditional breads, and seasoning dishes with ras el hanout, cumin, and saffron are common techniques.

Example Dishes

  • Moroccan Tagine: a slow-cooked stew combining meat, vegetables, fruits, and aromatic spices.
  • Couscous with Seven Vegetables: a traditional dish commonly prepared with steamed couscous and a variety of vegetables.
  • Shakshuka: eggs cooked in a spiced tomato and pepper sauce, enjoyed in different parts of North Africa.

Food and Traditions

Food plays an important role in hospitality and family gatherings across North Africa. Meals are often shared, with traditional recipes reflecting centuries of cultural exchange, trade, and regional ingredients.
