North African Cuisine

Food Culture

North African cooking blends Berber, Arab, Mediterranean, and Ottoman traditions. Meals are fragrant, warmly spiced, and often shared around a single platter of couscous.

Staples

Couscous, wheat bread, olives, dates, preserved lemons, and lamb.

Cooking Techniques

Slow cooking in a tagine pot, steaming couscous, and seasoning with ras el hanout, cumin, and saffron.

Example Dishes

  • Moroccan Tagine: slow-cooked meat and fruit stew.
  • Couscous with seven vegetables: a Friday family tradition.
