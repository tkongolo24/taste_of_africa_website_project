West African Cuisine

Discover the bold flavours, ingredients, and traditions of West Africa.

Food Culture

West African food is bold, generous, and meant for sharing. Meals revolve around communal pots, heat from scotch bonnet peppers, and deep, layered sauces.

Staples

Rice, yams, cassava, plantain, black-eyed peas, and palm oil are important ingredients across many West African cuisines.

Cooking Techniques

One-pot cooking, slow stewing, and open-fire grilling are common techniques. These methods help create the rich flavours found in dishes such as suya.

Example Dishes

  • Jollof Rice: a famous one-pot rice dish cooked with tomatoes, peppers, and spices.
  • Egusi Soup: a thick soup made with ground melon seeds and leafy greens.
  • Suya: seasoned grilled meat commonly enjoyed as street food.
