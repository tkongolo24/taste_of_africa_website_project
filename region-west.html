West African Cuisine

Food Culture

West African food is bold, generous, and meant for sharing. Meals revolve around communal pots, heat from scotch bonnet peppers, and deep, layered sauces.

Staples

Rice, yams, cassava, plantain, black-eyed peas, and palm oil.

Cooking Techniques

One-pot cooking, slow stewing, and open-fire grilling (as in suya).

Example Dishes

  • Jollof Rice: the region's famous one-pot rice dish.
  • Egusi soup: a thick soup of ground melon seeds and leafy greens.
