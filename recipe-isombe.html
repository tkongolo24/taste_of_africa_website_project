Isombe

Isombe is a traditional Rwandan dish of pounded cassava leaves simmered slowly with aubergine, spinach, and ground groundnuts. It is usually served with ugali, rice, or boiled bananas.

Ingredients

Servings: 4
  • 500 g pounded cassava leaves
  • 1 aubergine, diced
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 3 tbsp ground groundnuts (peanuts)
  • 2 tbsp palm or vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp salt

Instructions

Step 1: Boil the cassava leaves
  1. Boil the pounded leaves in water for 45 to 60 minutes until soft, then drain.
Step 2: Build the flavor
  1. Fry the onion and garlic in oil, add the aubergine, and cook until soft.
Step 3: Simmer and serve
  1. Add the leaves and the ground groundnuts with a cup of water.
  2. Simmer on low heat for 30 minutes, stirring often. Season and serve hot.
