Isombe

Isombe is a traditional Rwandan dish made from pounded cassava leaves that are slowly cooked until tender. It is often prepared with vegetables and groundnuts (peanuts), creating a rich and satisfying meal. Isombe is commonly served with ugali, rice or boiled bananas.

Ingredients

Adjust the serving size to calculate the ingredient quantities.

Servings: 4
  • 500 g pounded cassava leaves
  • 1 aubergine, diced
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3 tablespoons groundnuts (peanuts), finely ground
  • 2 tablespoons palm oil or vegetable oil
  • 250 ml water, plus extra if needed
  • 1 teaspoon salt, or to taste

Cooking Instructions

Step 1: Prepare the cassava leaves
  1. If using fresh cassava leaves, wash them thoroughly and remove any tough stems.
  2. Pound or finely chop the leaves until they have a smooth, consistent texture.
  3. Place the cassava leaves in a large pot with enough water to cover them.
  4. Bring to a boil and cook thoroughly, following a safe preparation method for cassava leaves, until completely tender.
Step 2: Prepare the vegetables
  1. Heat the palm oil or vegetable oil in a large cooking pot.
  2. Add the chopped onions and cook until softened.
  3. Add the minced garlic and stir for approximately one minute.
  4. Add the diced aubergine and cook until it begins to soften.
Step 3: Combine the ingredients
  1. Add the thoroughly cooked cassava leaves to the pot with the vegetables.
  2. Mix in the groundnuts.
  3. Add approximately one cup of water and stir everything together.
  4. Allow the mixture to simmer gently so the flavours combine.
Step 4: Simmer and season
  1. Continue cooking over low heat for approximately 30 minutes, stirring regularly.
  2. Add small amounts of water if the mixture becomes too thick.
  3. Add salt to taste and make sure the cassava leaves are fully cooked.
  4. Remove from the heat once the dish has a thick, creamy consistency.
Step 5: Serve

Serve Isombe hot with ugali, rice or boiled bananas for a traditional Rwandan meal.

Where It Comes From

Isombe is part of Rwanda's traditional food culture. Kigali, the capital city, is one place where visitors can experience Rwandan cuisine and traditional dishes.
