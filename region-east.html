East African Cuisine

Discover the diverse flavours, ingredients, and culinary traditions of East Africa.

Food Culture

East African food ranges from the fermented, spiced dishes of the Ethiopian highlands to the simple, hearty plates of the Great Lakes. Sharing food from a common platter is an important part of dining traditions across the region.

Staples

Teff, maize, cassava, beans, plantain, bananas, and cassava leaves are important ingredients in many East African cuisines.

Cooking Techniques

Fermentation, slow stewing with berbere spice, and charcoal grilling, including nyama choma, are popular cooking techniques across the region.

Example Dishes

  • Injera: Ethiopia's fermented teff flatbread, commonly served with different stews.
  • Isombe: a Rwandan dish made with cassava leaves, aubergine, and groundnuts.
  • Nyama Choma: grilled meat popular in Kenya and Tanzania, often served with side dishes.

Food and Traditions

Food in East Africa is closely connected to family, hospitality, celebrations, and community gatherings. Recipes and cooking traditions are passed between generations, helping preserve the region's cultural identity.
