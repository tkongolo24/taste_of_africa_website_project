East African Cuisine

Food Culture

East African food ranges from the fermented, spiced dishes of the Ethiopian highlands to the simple, hearty plates of the Great Lakes. Sharing from a common platter is central to the table.

Staples

Teff, maize, cassava, beans, plantain, and cassava leaves.

Cooking Techniques

Fermentation, slow stewing with berbere spice, and charcoal grilling (nyama choma).

Example Dishes

  • Injera: Ethiopia's fermented teff flatbread.
  • Isombe: Rwandan cassava leaves with aubergine and groundnuts.
